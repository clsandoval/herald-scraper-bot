# Herald Replay Quality — FINDINGS

Spike: distill "what makes a Dota 2 Herald-bracket replay worth reviewing on stream" from
streamer **Jenkins** (@JenkinsClips69) and his **"Herald Reviews"** series.

Status: THROWAWAY spike / first draft. Method-limited (see Confidence & Limitations).

---

## Method & source-access limitation (read first)

- Worked entirely from **video titles, search-result snippets, and third-party editorial
  pages** (Liquipedia, Hawk.live) plus **one primary-source tweet**.
- **Could NOT** read video transcripts, full YouTube descriptions, comments, or reddit
  threads. YouTube video/channel/playlist pages return only a nav/footer to the fetcher
  (consent wall); reddit is blocked to the crawler; the X post returned a paywall/HTTP error
  on direct fetch but the tweet text recurs verbatim in multiple search snippets.
- Therefore **the video titles are the primary evidence.** They are unusually
  self-describing for this series (Jenkins titles for the hook), so pattern inference is
  high-confidence, but exact per-episode content is unverified.
- All view-count / "most popular series" claims come from Liquipedia & Hawk.live, not
  independently verified.

**Primary-source selection criteria (the one hard quote):**
> "i will be watching herald replays tonight. if anyone is herald and wants ur replays
> really poorly analyzed on stream, hit me up. **the lower MMR the better, the crazier the
> game the better**" — @jenkinsdota (x.com/jenkinsdota/status/1411454523277824002)

Three explicit filters in that quote: (1) submitter must be **Herald** (bracket gate);
(2) **lower MMR is better** (hence the "10 MMR" branding — 10 MMR is the practical floor);
(3) **crazier is better** (chaos/absurdity over instructional value).

**The core bit / framing device:** he casts the lowest-tier games *as if they were
professional esports matches* — serious pro-caster analysis applied to play that doesn't
merit it. That comedic contrast is the payload (Liquipedia; Hawk.live). Recognized enough to
run on an official pro broadcast: "I did a Herald Review at DreamLeague" (with Sheever, BSJ,
Lacoste, reviewing the first Herald replay of patch 7.38).

## What he features, ranked by frequency/prominence

Ranked by how often the theme recurs across collected titles and how central it is to the
series' identity. Example titles are verbatim (evidence).

### 1. Extreme-low-MMR / "cursed" framing — the baseline gate (very high)
Almost everything is branded by how low/pure the bracket is. "10 MMR" is a recurring brand.
- "Reviewing a CURSED Herald 10 MMR game with Jenkins"
- "I watched a 10 MMR Pudge and Techies go to war"
- "This is a real inventory from this 10 MMR game"
- "Einstein is alive, and he plays in 10 MMR"
- "The most confusing 10 MMR replay I've ever watched"
- "FINALLY! No smurfs in this 2 hour Herald game"  <- purity: no smurf contamination

### 2. Chaotic / marathon-length games (very high — a signature type)
Extreme game length + total chaos is a top selection axis ("crazier the better").
- "The most sadistic Herald game I've ever seen" (3+ hr, "broke Dota")
- "Mega creeps vs mega creeps in Herald, for 2 hours"
- "Herald Warlock jungles for 70 minutes"
- "Herald forgets dropped Rapier for 2 hours"
- "The most confusing 10 MMR replay I've ever watched" (~2 hr, 220+ kills reported)
- "I showed Purge a 2 hour Herald game"

### 3. Troll / unorthodox / "degenerate" item builds & strats (high)
Directly descends from Jenkins' origin as an unorthodox-build guide maker.
- "Ultimate terrorist build discovered in Herald"
- "Herald Dota is the best drug on Earth" (Armlet + Dagon + Terrorblade)
- "This is a real inventory from this 10 MMR game" (PA crits ~10,000; ~20 Divine Rapiers)
- "Einstein is alive, and he plays in 10 MMR" (Drow, 0 skill points, Kaya/Yasha->Rapier)
- "Abusing the most bonkers strat (in Herald)"
- "Why does this actually work?!" (Herald 2 Techies "invents a meta")

### 4. Absurd/extreme stat lines & spectacle stats (high)
Single legible numeric hook (deaths, kills, crit value, rapier count).
- "I watched a Herald feed 43 deaths ... then win." <- feed->win throw/comeback combo
- "nature's prophet died 250 times"; "Herald spends 40 minutes dead, then wins"
- "The most insane Herald builds of all time" (Luna gets 9 Blood Grenades; ES 1-shots)
- "70 kill Snapfire vs 70 kill Slark (in Herald)"; "What getting 60 kills looks like"
- 220+ kill games, ~10,000 crits, ~20 rapiers (see above)

### 5. Hard/unusual heroes played by Heralds (high)
Difficulty-mismatch comedy: Herald attempts a mechanically brutal hero.
- "I watched a Herald play the hardest hero in Dota"
- "Actual freak plays Techies (in Herald)" (takes game hostage)
- "The most mentally ill Techies in Dota history"
- "Herald with 2000 Pudge games is insane genius" (one-trick / obsessive mastery)
- "Herald Sniper tests the limits of Dota 2"

### 6. Wrong-role / unconventional dominance ("genius") (medium-high)
A Herald doing something absurd that *works*, framed as accidental genius.
- "Heralds think on a level we don't" (carry Warlock dominates ~2 hr game)
- "The most genius Herald game I've ever seen"
- "The best carry in Dota that nobody expected"
- "Heralds have solved Dota" / "Herald rejects the rules of Dota" / "Insanity or genius?"

### 7. Team griefing / behavior-score / abandons / "allies are the enemy" (medium)
Comedic team self-destruction + very-low-behavior-score lobbies.
- "In Herald, allies are your greatest enemies"
- "What does 0 behavior score Herald look like?"
- "Low priority Herald is extremely cursed"
- "In Herald, hotkeys are the enemy"

### 8. Epic throws / comebacks / smurf-loses (medium — often fused with #4)
Big legible lead reversals are prime; smurf-who-still-loses is a recurring sub-hook.
- "Herald game with more throws than basketball" (loses with 60k gold lead)
- "The most devastating failed 322 in Dota history" (deliberate throw gone wrong)
- "I watched a Herald feed 43 deaths ... then win."
- "I watched a smurf get 50 kills in Herald... then lose!?"; "I watched a 115 winstreak
  smurf lose in Herald"  <- smurf loss = comedic reversal (note: smurf-present, see purity)
- Mega-creep standoffs (see #2) are comeback/throw fodder.

### 9. Wholesome / character-driven picks (low-medium — occasional palate cleanser)
- "I watched a 63 year old dad's 10 MMR match"
- "Herald Clockwork learns valuable life lessons"

### 10. Collabs / cross-overs (format variant, not a game-selection axis)
- "I did a Herald Review at DreamLeague"; "I did a Herald review with Gorgc";
  "HERALD REVIEW LIVE WITH GUNNAR"; "HERALD REVIEW WITH GIRLFRIEND!!!"

**Negative signal:** searches for **wholesome/toxic/all-chat-drama** surfaced almost no
titles. Toxicity/wholesomeness appear *incidental*, not a primary selection axis. The axis is
**comedy/spectacle/absurdity**, not moral drama.

**Watch-out (source hygiene):** a few surfaced titles are Jenkins' OWN gameplay, not viewer
Herald reviews — e.g. "The most despicable strat in Dota 2" (his Shadow Fiend offlane build)
and "playing stupid heroes that grief in 10k+ MMR". Don't treat those as Herald-review
selection signals.

## Computable vs subjective — signal ledger

For each recurring signal, whether a ranking agent could score it from stored match data
(OpenDota / Stratz fields: KDA, GPM/XPM, item purchases + timings, net-worth graph, hero id,
role/lane, deaths, duration, rank tier, ability builds, per-minute series).

| # | Signal | Computable? | How (fields) | Confidence signal->Jenkins |
|---|--------|-------------|--------------|---------------------------|
| 1 | Herald / very-low bracket | **Yes** | `rank_tier`/`avg_rank_tier` = Herald; lower is better | High |
| 1 | Pure (no smurfs) | **Partial** | no direct smurf flag; proxy = low account age / low game count / rank uncertainty. Noisy. | Medium |
| 2 | Marathon length | **Yes** | `duration` (flag >90 min, strongly >120 min) | High |
| 2 | Total chaos / high kill count | **Yes** | sum of kills both teams; kills-per-minute; teamfight density | High |
| 2 | Mega-creep / long standoff | **Partial** | very long `duration` + barracks destroyed early but game continues; net worth staying close late | Medium |
| 3 | Troll item build | **Yes (heuristic)** | item purchases vs hero norms: wrong-stat items (Radiance/Dagon on wrong hero), duplicate stacking (multiple Rapiers), no-boots-late | Medium-High |
| 3 | Unorthodox strat | **Partial** | proxies only (lane assignment, jungle farm share, role vs hero) | Medium |
| 4 | Absurd stat lines | **Yes** | deaths >= ~25 for one player; a 0-many player; >2 Divine Rapiers; 200+ combined kills | High |
| 5 | Hard hero attempted | **Yes** | `hero_id` in {Meepo, Invoker, Chen, Techies, Arc Warden, Visage, ...} in Herald | High |
| 5 | One-trick / obsession | **Partial** | needs player match history (games-on-hero count) — extra API call | Medium |
| 6 | Wrong-role dominance | **Yes (heuristic)** | hero off its usual role/lane AND top net worth/GPM on team | Medium-High |
| 6 | "Genius"/insane individual play | **No (mostly)** | judgment is subjective; only proxy via outlier GPM/XPM/KDA-for-bracket | Low-Medium |
| 7 | Griefing / abandons | **Partial** | `leaver_status`/abandons; very low actions; repeated deaths to same source | Medium |
| 7 | Low behavior score / low priority | **Partial** | not exposed per-match by OpenDota; sometimes on player profile (Stratz). Proxy via abandons/comms. "0 behavior score Herald" is a real hook. | Medium |
| 8 | Throw / comeback | **Yes** | net-worth-graph lead reversal: max lead for the losing side; barracks/mega state | High |
| 8 | Deliberate throw / failed 322 | **No (mostly)** | intent to throw isn't in data; only proxy = massive lead lost (see Throw) | Low |
| 8 | Smurf-who-loses | **Partial** | one clear outlier player (high kills/GPM/APM) on the LOSING team = comedic. Uses same smurf proxy as PURITY, inverted. | Medium |
| - | Comedic contrast (the payload) | **No** | inherently subjective humor — the core appeal is NOT computable | (uncomputable) |
| - | Chat toxicity / wholesomeness | **No** | chat/all-chat not reliably in match data; sentiment subjective | (uncomputable) |
| - | Story/character (63-yo dad) | **No** | comes from the submitter's note, not match data | (uncomputable) |

**Bottom line:** length, kill-chaos, extreme stat lines, low bracket, hard-hero picks,
duplicate-rapier/troll-item heuristics, and net-worth throw/comeback detection are all
**computable** and map well to Jenkins' stated + observed filters. The *humor payload itself*
(why it's funny, chat drama, human-interest story) is **not** computable — a scorer can only
surface strong candidates; a human still picks the winner.

## Confidence summary

- **High confidence:** the three stated criteria (Herald gate, lower-MMR-better,
  crazier-better); the marathon-length and absurd-stat-line themes; that comedic contrast is
  the framing device. Evidenced by the verbatim tweet + many self-describing titles.
- **Medium confidence:** relative ranking of themes 3-9 (inferred from title frequency, not
  from watching or from per-video view data).
- **Low confidence / unverified:** the exact submission pipeline (only "hit me up" on
  stream/Twitter confirmed; no form/Discord verified); per-video content; whether "genius"
  episodes are outlier-stat detectable vs purely editorial.
